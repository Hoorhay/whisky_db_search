Score Guide

95 – 100 Absolute must, dream dram
90 – <95 Great whisky, real joy, for the alone moments
85 – <90 Very good whisky, enjoyable when sipping
80 – <85 Good whisky, pleasant but forgettable
70 – <80 Average whisky, utility, good choice for getting drunk
60 – <70 Drinkable, basic stuff, regrettable choice for getting drunk
50 – <60 Barely drinkable, unforgiving neat
 0 – <50 Terrible, pour it down the sink
